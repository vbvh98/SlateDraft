Local-first privacy: your scripts never leave your machine.

Production houses and writers' rooms work under strict NDAs. SlateDraft Studio runs entirely on your local filesystem with zero cloud sync, zero user accounts, and zero subscriptions.

  • Zero cloud uploads. Every .slatedraft, .fdx, and .fountain file is read and written directly to your disk.
  • Zero telemetry or accounts. No login wall, no analytics beacons, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no monthly seat fees.
  • Strict desktop CSP. The Tauri v2 window enforces default-src 'self' to block remote scripts and unauthorized outbound requests.
