Local-first privacy: your scripts never leave your machine.

Production houses and writers' rooms operate under strict NDAs. SlateDraft Studio runs entirely on your local filesystem with zero cloud sync, zero user accounts, and zero subscriptions.

  • Zero cloud uploads. Every .slatedraft, .fdx, and .fountain file is read and written atomically to your local disk with automatic rolling backups.
  • Zero telemetry or accounts. No login wall, no analytics beacons, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no recurring seat fees.
  • Strict desktop CSP. The Tauri v2 window enforces default-src 'self' to block remote scripts and unauthorized outbound connections.
